AimPool Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

AimPool: Billiards Shot Coach ("AimPool") is designed to analyze pool-table photos on your device.

Summary

  • AimPool processes imported or captured table photos on-device.
  • AimPool does not upload your photos to a server.
  • AimPool does not require accounts.
  • AimPool does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. AimPool never sees your payment-card or billing details.
  • The built-in sample table and the Learn lessons are free; camera and library photo analysis, and unlimited Learn chalk, require AimPool Premium.
  • AimPool contacts no server of its own; its only network use is Apple StoreKit.

Photos and Camera

AimPool lets you choose a photo from your photo library or capture a table photo with the camera. The app uses the image to support table calibration, ball review, and shot recommendation inside the app. Photo analysis runs on-device, and photos are not uploaded by AimPool.

Saved Analyses

AimPool no longer keeps a list of past analyses. Earlier versions saved each completed analysis to a History list stored only on your device: the table photo (for your own photos), the calibration corners, the reviewed balls, the selected rules mode, and the computed recommendation. This version deletes that list and its stored photos from your device the first time it opens. None of it was ever uploaded, synced, or shared by AimPool.

Purchases

AimPool Premium is offered as an annual auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple StoreKit. The intended U.S. base price is $19.99 per year; the app displays Apple's StoreKit-localized price for the customer's storefront. Eligible customers may receive a free trial, whose length the App Store shows before purchase. After the trial, or immediately if the customer is not eligible, the subscription renews annually until canceled. Price and trial availability depend on the storefront and live App Store configuration. Apple processes payment and billing information. AimPool receives StoreKit product, introductory-offer eligibility, and verified entitlement status, but does not receive payment-card numbers or Apple account billing details.

Customers can manage or cancel the subscription through their Apple account. Existing customers with a verified legacy lifetime purchase continue to receive AimPool Premium without an annual subscription.

Learn Progress

The Learn lessons keep your progress on your device only: the lessons you have finished, your best first-try score in each, your experience points, the days you practised, and your chalk. Nothing of it is uploaded, synced, or shared by AimPool.

Free Sample And Premium Access

AimPool's free mode lets you explore the built-in sample table and the Learn lessons, whose new lessons are paced by chalk. Camera capture, photo-library import, analysis of your own table photos, and unlimited chalk require AimPool Premium.

Network

AimPool does not contact a server of its own. Its only network use is Apple StoreKit, for purchases, subscription status, and restoring purchases.

Analytics and Tracking

AimPool does not include analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, tracking SDKs, App Tracking Transparency prompts, or IDFA access. AimPool does not sell personal data.

Feedback and Reviews

Settings › Send Feedback opens your mail app with a message to the AimPool team. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you write, including the short details block (app and iOS version, phone model, game, whether Premium is on, and your language), which you can delete before sending. We use it only to reply and to improve AimPool. AimPool may also ask, through Apple's own prompt, for an App Store rating; the days you used the app and the moments that lead to that prompt are counted on your device only.
