AimPool Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

AimPool: Billiards Shot Coach ("AimPool") is designed to analyze pool-table photos on your device.

Summary

  • AimPool processes imported or captured table photos on-device.
  • AimPool does not upload your photos to a server.
  • AimPool does not require accounts.
  • AimPool does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. AimPool never sees payment-card or billing details.
  • The built-in sample table and the Learn lessons are free; camera and library photo analysis, and unlimited Learn chalk, require AimPool Premium.
  • AimPool contacts no server of its own; its only network use is Apple StoreKit.

Photos and camera

AimPool lets you choose a photo from your library or capture a table photo with the camera. The image supports table calibration, ball review, and shot recommendations inside the app. Photo analysis runs on-device, and AimPool does not upload those photos.

Saved analyses

AimPool no longer keeps a list of past analyses. Earlier versions saved each completed analysis to a History list stored only on your device: the table photo (for your own photos), the calibration corners, the reviewed balls, the selected rules mode, and the computed recommendation. The current version deletes that list and its stored photos from your device the first time it opens. None of it was ever uploaded, synced, or shared by AimPool.

Purchases

AimPool Premium is offered as an annual auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple through StoreKit. The intended U.S. price is $19.99 per year; Apple displays the localized price for your storefront before purchase. Eligible customers may receive a 3-day free trial. After the trial, or immediately if you are not eligible, the subscription renews annually until canceled.

Apple processes payment and billing information. AimPool receives StoreKit product information, introductory-offer eligibility, and verified entitlement status, but does not receive payment-card numbers or Apple account billing details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Customers with a verified legacy AimPool Premium lifetime purchase keep Premium access without an annual subscription.

Learn progress

The Learn lessons keep your progress on your device only: the lessons you have finished, your best first-try score in each, your experience points, the days you practised, and your chalk. None of it is uploaded, synced, or shared by AimPool.

Free sample and premium access

AimPool's free mode lets you explore the built-in sample table and the Learn lessons, whose new lessons are paced by chalk. Camera capture, photo-library import, analysis of your own table photos, and unlimited chalk require AimPool Premium.

Network

AimPool does not contact a server of its own. Its only network use is Apple StoreKit, for purchases, subscription status, and restoring purchases. Links you choose to open, such as this page, open in your browser.

Analytics and tracking

AimPool does not include analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, tracking SDKs, an App Tracking Transparency prompt, or IDFA access. AimPool does not sell personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the AimPool support page.
